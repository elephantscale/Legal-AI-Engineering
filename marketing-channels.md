# Marketing and Distribution Channels — Legal AI Engineering

**Compiled:** 2026-11-11 · **Companion to:** `job-market-research.md`
**Scope:** where this course can be distributed and promoted, what each channel costs, and a
90-day experiment with a kill criterion. Partner facts come from `ES/es-marketing`; external
facts are cited.

> **The finding that should shape everything below.** The course is **already published and
> for sale by a partner**, globally, and we have not yet sent that partner the outline we
> promised them. We do not have a price of our own, a public page of our own, or a delivered
> cohort. **Fix the shop window before buying traffic.** Every paid option in this document
> is wasted money until there is somewhere for a click to land.

## 2. Channels assessed

### 2.1 Elephant Scale's own channel — 22 resellers *(strongest, already paid for)*

**NobleProg is Mark's channel** (confirmed 2026-11-11), and it is the most active partner on
the list — four contacts, two ES courses already published with live dates and prices, and this
course already listed.

**What it is:** a verified list of 20 reseller account managers plus two LearnQuest catalog
coordinators, on a monthly one-screen note cadence; NobleProg (×4 contacts), LearnQuest,
ProTech, Judge Group, SimpliLearn, Accelebrate, Web Age Solutions, Logical Operations, RX-M,
FastLane and others. Source: `es-marketing/drafts/channel-note-2026-11.md`.

**Cost:** zero. **Reach:** indirect but qualified — these people have clients asking for AI
training now. **Proof it works:** NobleProg turned a catalog entry into live listings with live
dates in weeks.

**What it needs from us:** the outline owed by 2026-11-19, and a decision on whether a partner
may advertise the certificate. Nothing else. **This is the highest-return action in this
document and it costs one email.**

### 2.2 ACEDS *(best fit for a free webinar to a new audience)*

- Part of **BARBRI Global**; the certifying body for CEDS, the eDiscovery credential our
  audience's colleagues hold.
- Runs **free educational webinars with named sponsors** — e.g. a December 3 2026 session
  sponsored by Trust Array, and a September 2026 session on AI in document review featuring
  PwC and Relativity. This is a standing, repeatable format, not a one-off.
- Has an **Affiliate Partner Program**, used in August 2026 to admit DecoverAI, an AI
  legal-discovery platform. Explicitly positioned to connect certified specialists with vendors.
- Sells its own self-paced **eDiscovery Technology Certificate at $295** — a useful read on what
  this audience pays for self-serve credentials, and a long way below a $2,700 instructor-led course.
- ILTA members get **20% off ACEDS training**, via an ILTA–BARBRI partnership that includes
  joint programming at ILTA conferences.

**Verdict: the best route to an audience we do not already reach**, and the right *second*
webinar. The audience is exactly ours (eDiscovery practitioners and the technologists who support
them), the format already exists, and the credential adjacency is real — we are proposing a
*defensibility* talk to the body that certifies defensibility. It goes second only because
NobleProg costs nothing, hosts registration, and has dates to convert into; ACEDS brings reach
but no booking path.
Sources: [aceds.org webinars](https://aceds.org/upcoming-webinars/) · [DecoverAI affiliate announcement](https://aceds.org/aceds-welcomes-decoverai-as-newest-affiliate-partner-aceds-blog/) · [eDTech certificate](https://aceds.org/ediscovery-technology-certificate/) · [ILTA–ACEDS](https://www.iltanet.org/resources/aceds-ilta)

### 2.3 ILTA *(right audience, wrong price for now)*

- **Business-partner tiers, 2027, annual:** ILTAMAX **$145,425**, Titanium **$95,550**, Platinum
  **$45,150**, Gold **$23,100**, Silver **$17,325**, **ILTA365 $6,300**.
- **ILTACON 2027:** Nashville, Gaylord Opryland, **August 8–12 2027** per most sources; one
  third-party listing says Orlando, August 22–26 — **confirm with ILTA before any plan depends
  on it**. Speaker applications historically open **mid-to-late March**.
- A **suitcasing policy** effectively bars commercial activity at the conference by
  non-sponsors, and attendees are told not to accept materials from non-sponsors.
- ILTA also runs a **careers board** — which is where several of the postings in
  `job-market-research.md` surfaced. Worth watching as market intelligence, not as a channel.

**Verdict:** ILTA365 at **$6,300** is the only tier that makes sense before there is revenue,
and even that is a year's budget for an unproven course. **Defer. Revisit after a delivered
cohort**, and aim at the **March 2027 speaker window** — a speaking slot is the cheap way in,
and the job-market map is a speakable topic.
Sources: [ILTA business partner opportunities](https://www.iltanet.org/get-involved/bpopps) · [ILTACON 2027](https://shaer.io/at/iltacon-2027/) · [ILTA careers](https://careers.iltanet.org/jobseekers/index.cfm)

### 2.4 CLOC *(wrong buyer)*

- **CLOC Global Institute 2027:** ARIA, Las Vegas, **May 10–13 2027**. Sponsorship sales open
  **December 8**; priority-point sales **November 9**. An **"Education sponsor"** slot exists;
  terms and price **Unknown**. Contact `sponsorship@cloc.org`. A third-party directory estimates
  2,000–2,500 attendees.
- Theme: *The Human Edge*, with a "Practical" track on AI, agents and automation in legal ops.

**Verdict: the weakest fit of the four associations.** CLOC is legal **operations** — buyers and
managers of legal work, not builders of it. They would be a market for the adoption material we
do not have (gap #5), not for eleven Python labs. Note and skip.
Sources: [CLOC become a sponsor](https://globalinstitute.cloc.org/become-a-sponsor/) · [program overview](https://globalinstitute.cloc.org/program-overview/)

### 2.5 LawSites / LawNext — Bob Ambrogi *(best paid test, if any)*

- Available formats: **banner and box ads** on LawSites and LegalTech Monitor; **sponsored
  posts**; **company profile pages**; **audio spots on the LawNext podcast and Legaltech Week**;
  a LawNext PR podcast; enhanced directory listings.
- **Rates: Unknown** — not published. Inquiries historically to `info@lawnext.com` (producer:
  Populus Radio).
- **Audience size: Unknown.** The publisher's own claim is that the audience is almost entirely
  legal-tech people. No independent figures exist.

**Verdict:** the highest-signal paid option in legal tech, and the only one plausibly affordable.
But **ask for rates before planning around it**, and treat a *sponsored post* as the product —
an article version of the job-market research is far more likely to be read, and to be picked up
editorially, than a banner.
Sources: [How to advertise on LawSites](https://www.lawnext.com/advertise) · [sponsorship opportunities post](https://www.lawnext.com/2024/03/new-sponsorship-and-content-opportunities-available-on-our-lawsites-and-lawnext-group-of-properties.html)

### 2.6 LinkedIn *(free, and the natural home for the research)*

Organic posting from Mark's own account, where the FreeEed and *Weekly AI Advances* credentials
already live. **Cost zero, attribution poor, ceiling low on its own** — but it is where legal-tech
people argue about exactly this, and the job-market map is a post that does not read as a pitch.
Treat it as the distribution surface for the content asset, not as a channel in itself. **Do not
buy LinkedIn ads** — B2B training CPMs there are high and our landing page does not exist yet.

### 2.7 Reframe.lawyer *(new, candidate-side, worth watching)*

A self-described *"talent and market intelligence platform for legal AI"* — curated legal-AI job
board (~100 roles including the Dentons *Legal AI Engineer*), a weekly newsletter, a "Market
Pulse" hiring-data product, a **free on-demand masterclass on transitioning into legal AI**, and a
"Get Matched" service. Nothing published for training providers.

**Verdict:** their audience *is* our student — engineers and lawyers moving into legal AI — and
they are already running a free masterclass, which is the format we would want. **Low-cost
approach worth making**: offer the job-market map as a guest piece or a co-run session. No money
should change hands at this stage.
Source: [reframe.lawyer/jobs](https://reframe.lawyer/jobs)

### 2.8 Direct — the first-student channel

Carlene Gordon (meeting 2026-11-17) is the first cohort and the first case study. One delivered
student with a capstone repository and a certificate is worth more in every channel above than any
amount of description. **This is the proof point the other channels are waiting on.**

## 4. "Advertise or free webinar?" — the answer is webinar

Mark's 2026-11-11 email asks a partner's customer-service inbox exactly this question. The answer
from this research is **free webinar, and not yet advertising** — for four reasons:

1. **There is nowhere for an ad to send people.** No ES page for this course, no published price,
   no scheduled dates — a dry run of Day 1 has still never been run. The only public page is a
   partner's, and it is already out of date. Advertising into that is paying to find out we were
   not ready.
2. **The free educational webinar is the native format of this market.** ACEDS runs them with
   sponsors as a standing series; Reframe already runs a free masterclass; the ILTA calendar is
   built from them. Nobody in legal tech buys a two-day engineering course from a banner.
3. **A webinar produces the two assets we lack** — a recording, and a registrant list we own.
   Advertising produces neither.
4. **We now have a topic that is genuinely new** rather than promotional: *what six Legal AI
   Engineer job postings actually require* — including the finding that the title is JD-gated at
   four of six employers while the best-paid matching role is called something else. That is a
   talk a programme chair wants and an audience shares. The course is the footnote.

**On the email as sent.** A generic `customerservice` inbox is the weakest door into any of
these organisations. Every one of them routes this kind of request to a **named** team —
`sponsor@iltanet.org` and `events@iltanet.org` at ILTA, `sponsorship@cloc.org` at CLOC,
`info@lawnext.com` at LawNext, the Affiliate Partner Program at ACEDS.

**And if it went to NobleProg, the answer to "who should I talk to" is Chandra Tan.** She asked
for detailed outlines on 2026-11-05 precisely so she could build out the web pages; she is the
one who published the two July listings; and she already has this course live. Customer service
will route that email slowly or not at all. A direct note to Chandra does three jobs in one
message: delivers the outline owed by 2026-11-19, corrects her stale page, and proposes the
webinar — and a partner who is already selling the course has a commercial reason to say yes.

**The strongest version of that email is not "advertise or webinar?" but "I have research on
legal-AI hiring and a 45-minute session built on it — who runs your education calendar?"** The
first asks them to do work; the second hands them a slot filler.

## 5. Proposed 90-day experiment (2026-11-11 → 2027-02-09)

One hypothesis per phase, one number that decides it, and a kill criterion. No spend before
Phase 3, and Phase 3 only happens if Phase 2 clears.

### Phase 1 — Days 1–14 (to 2026-11-25): fix the shop window. $0

| Action | Done when |
|---|---|
| Send Chandra Tan the Legal AI Engineering outline (**owed 2026-11-19**), noting the 2026-11-15 split: a 2-day Core and a separately scheduled 1-day Deep Dive, Module 7 in the Deep Dive, electives on request | Email sent; her page updated |
| Reconcile the NobleProg/Govtra page against current `outline.md`; list every divergence | Written diff exists |
| Decide whether a partner may advertise the **Legal AI Engineer Certificate**, and under what name (`DEC-015`) | Decision recorded |
| Settle the audience sentence — engineers, or engineers *and* lawyers (see `job-market-research.md` §4) | Decision recorded |
| Ask LawSites for a rate card. Ask nothing else of anyone. | Reply received or chased once |

**Gate:** Phase 2 does not start until the partner page is accurate. **Measure:** divergences
closed; one correct public listing.

### Phase 2 — Days 15–45 (to 2026-12-26): one content asset, three free distributions. $0

1. **Publish the job-market map** — the §1–§3 content of `job-market-research.md`, rewritten for
   a public reader: *"What six Legal AI Engineer job postings actually ask for."* Honest, cited,
   not a pitch. It ends with one line about the course.
2. **Distribute it three ways:** LinkedIn (Mark's account), a tip to Bob Ambrogi at LawSites (he
   covers the legal-AI job market editorially — **a free mention is worth more than a paid one**),
   and an offer to Reframe.lawyer as a guest piece.
3. **Run one free webinar, co-branded with NobleProg** — pitched to Chandra Tan in the same
   message as the outline, not to a customer-service queue. The template already exists in
   `es-marketing/drafts/webinar-nobleprog-sais100.md`, and its shape is right: a live demo where
   a **number moves**, not slides. The legal version is the one that lands — score a review
   pipeline, break egress, watch **LEGAL-A** fire and the verdict flip to Do Not Deploy, repair
   it, re-score. NobleProg co-brands, promotes to their list and hosts registration, exactly as
   proposed for the July launch; they have live dates to convert registrations into, which no
   other channel does. **Pitch ACEDS the same session as the second run** once there is a
   recording and a registration count to show.
4. **Carlene's delivery happens in this window** if dates land. Capture the capstone artifact and
   one quotable sentence from her, with permission.

**Measure:** ≥1 editorial pickup *or* ≥1 webinar slot agreed; ≥10 inbound inquiries; registrant
list started. **Kill criterion:** if the research piece gets no pickup and no webinar slot in 30
days, the problem is the offer or the audience, not the budget — **do not proceed to Phase 3.**

### Phase 3 — Days 46–90 (to 2027-02-09): exactly one paid test. Budget ≤ $5,000

Run the webinar. Then **one** paid placement, chosen on Phase 2 evidence:

- **If the research piece performed:** a LawSites **sponsored post** version of it (rate TBC).
- **If the webinar performed:** sponsor an **ACEDS educational webinar** slot in their standing series.
- **If both stalled:** spend nothing. Reassess the offer, and consider that the addressable buyer
  may be a **platform partner** (the Merlin conversation) rather than an audience at all.

**Measure:** cost per qualified inquiry, and one hard number — **enrolments or partner-delivered
seats**. **Threshold to continue into Q2:** ≥3 paid seats or ≥1 partner-scheduled delivery from
90 days of effort. Below that, the channel is not the constraint and more spend will not fix it.

### What this experiment deliberately does not do

- **No ILTA sponsorship**, at any tier, inside 90 days.
- **No paid search or paid social**, at any budget.
- **No conference booth.** The March 2027 ILTACON speaker window is the conference play, and it
  is free.
- **No second webinar** until the first one's registrant list has been contacted.
